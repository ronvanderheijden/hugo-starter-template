You will find us at Main Street 1, right in the centre of town.

## Opening hours

We are open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 17:00, and on Saturday mornings.

## Getting here

There is bicycle parking at the door. Bus lines 3 and 7 stop around the corner, and paid car parking is available two streets away on Market Square.

{{< alert type="warning" title="Roadworks" >}}
Main Street is partly closed for roadworks this summer — follow the yellow signs from Market Square.
