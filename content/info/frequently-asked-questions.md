The questions we hear most often, answered in one place.

## Do I need to be a member?

No — most of our activities are open to everyone. Members do get discounts and can vote at the annual meeting.

## How do I become a member?

Send us a message through the [contact page](/contact/) and we will take care of the rest. Membership runs per calendar year.

## Can I volunteer?

Absolutely. We are always looking for helping hands, whether for a single afternoon or a regular role.
