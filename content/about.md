Example Organisation was founded by a handful of neighbours who wanted a place where people could meet, learn, and lend each other a hand. Today we are a small team of staff and volunteers serving our local community.

## What we stand for

We keep things simple: everyone is welcome, activities should be affordable, and decisions are made together with our members.

## The team

Our day-to-day work is carried by volunteers, supported by a small board that meets once a month. Want to join us? We are always looking for helping hands.
